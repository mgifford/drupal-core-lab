   Observe: After: open state and the chosen width persist after reload.

   Observe: After: Navigation stays expanded. Below 1280px a click that opens one panel closes the other on both sides, so both are open only after a reload from stored state; on Before that reload collapses Navigation.

9. **each:** Narrow the frame below 1024px and open the sidebar.
