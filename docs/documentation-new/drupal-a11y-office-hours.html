Drupal Accessibility office hours are a monthly opportunity to ask accessibility questions, discuss barriers, and review work on Drupal core, contributed projects, themes, documentation, and sites. Anyone working with Drupal accessibility is welcome.

Schedule

Office hours are held on the third Thursday of each month. The meeting time alternates to include more time zones:

  • Even-numbered months: 15:00 UTC in February, April, June, August, October, and December.
  • Odd-numbered months: 20:00 UTC in January, March, May, July, September, and November.

Check the Drupal Core calendar before joining. The calendar is the authoritative source for meeting dates, local-time conversion, cancellations, and schedule changes.

Prepare for office hours

Add your topic to the office hours agenda. Include:

  • a link to the Drupal.org issue, project, merge request, documentation page, or demonstration;
  • a short description of the accessibility question or barrier;
  • the people and tasks affected; and
  • the decision, testing, or review you need.

Do not put credentials, personal information, or other sensitive material in the public agenda. If the meeting format creates a participation barrier, raise it in the Drupal Slack #accessibility channel before the meeting without disclosing private details publicly.

Join and follow up

  • Join the Google Meet
  • Add or review agenda items
  • Read previous meeting notes
  • Join Drupal Slack and use the #accessibility channel

Office hours provide volunteer guidance and review. They do not guarantee immediate approval, issue resolution, or a maintainer decision. Record findings, decisions, test results, and follow-up work in the relevant Drupal.org issue or project so that the project record remains public and durable.
